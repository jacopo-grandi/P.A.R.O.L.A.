## How to read the numbers

- **Fidelity WER** is the primary metric (decision 0010): it counts whether the words are the ones spoken, without rewording; fillers, repetitions, truncated words and false starts do not count. **subst.** = substitutions as a % of the words: the direct sign of rewording.
- **Literal WER**: only fillers are removed; repetitions and truncated words count.
- **RTF**: processing time / audio duration, on the Mac (M4 Pro).
- **Reproducibility**: fixed MLX random seed (0). Whisper's temperature fallback samples at random: without a seed, two runs of the same system give different numbers.
- **Silver references**: words checked by a person, fillers mostly proposed by Whisper large-v3-turbo. The filler metrics are optimistic for the turbo variants, especially with the prompt.
- **Anchoring**: the references start from the large-v3-turbo draft, so the turbo systems have an advantage over the other models.
- With few references the intervals are wide: differences within the intervals are not significant.
